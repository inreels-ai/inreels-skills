# Feature 3D

Modern animated-feature look: appealing characters, cinematic light.

![pixar-3d](../examples/styles/pixar-3d.jpg)

```
Modern feature-animation 3D render: appealing stylised character with large eyes and soft rounded forms, subsurface scattering in the skin, physically based materials with believable cloth weave and micro-detail, cinematic key light with warm rim light, shallow depth of field, polished studio-quality global illumination
```

**Best for** family stories, mascots, premium brand pieces.

**Notes**
- Unlike the material styles, this one needs **no negative clause** — it is a
  render look, and the lighting terms carry it.
- "Subsurface scattering" and "rim light" do most of the work. Keep both.
- Holds a character across shots better than the photoreal pack, because the
  design is simpler.
