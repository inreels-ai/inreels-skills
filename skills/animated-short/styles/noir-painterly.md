# Noir painterly

Storm-lit oil painting. Heavy weather, one warm light source in a cold frame.

```
Painterly noir animation, thick visible oil-paint brushwork on textured canvas, storm-lit chiaroscuro, deep muted teal shadows and sea, warm lantern amber as the only bright accent, heavy rain streaks, soft film grain, vertical 9:16 framing
```

**Best for** drama, mystery, character stories, anything at night or at sea.

**Proven on** "The Last Watch" — a full 33s film, 8 shots, character held
across every one. See `../examples/`.

**Notes**
- The single warm accent is what makes it read as noir rather than murk. Keep
  exactly one light source in the story.
- Holds a character well across shots when driven from a reference sheet.
- Faces come out expressive; this is a good pack for a story with one hero.
