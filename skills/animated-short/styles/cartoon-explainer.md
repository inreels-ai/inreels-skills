# Cartoon explainer

![cartoon-explainer](../examples/styles/cartoon-explainer.jpg)

Flat bold vector. The style for facts, not feelings.

```
Bright flat vector cartoon, bold clean outlines of even weight, simple shapes, cheerful saturated palette, minimal shading, friendly rounded character design, plain uncluttered background
```

**Best for** explainers, how-things-work, science facts, anything for kids.

**Notes**
- Pairs with word-by-word captions rather than the lower-thirds a story uses.
- The plain background is doing work — it keeps the subject readable at phone
  size and leaves room for on-screen numbers.
- A mascot in this style stays consistent more easily than a realistic
  character, because there is less face to drift.
